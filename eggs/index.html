Questions about our eggs

How are your eggs sold?

By the dozen. Tell us how many dozen you'd like on the order form and we'll confirm availability and price.

How much do they cost?

Prices can change with the season, so we confirm the current price when we reply to your order request.

Can I cook duck eggs like chicken eggs?

Yes. You can fry, scramble or bake with them. Because they're bigger, many bakers use about two duck eggs for every three chicken eggs in a recipe.

I'm allergic to chicken eggs. Can I eat duck eggs?

Don't assume so. Some people who react to chicken eggs also react to duck eggs. Please check with your doctor first.
