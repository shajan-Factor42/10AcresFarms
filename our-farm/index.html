Our Farm

A small family farm in Gwinnett County

We raise chickens and ducks on our family farm and sell their eggs to neighbors across Gwinnett County.

Who we are

10 Acre Farms is run by our family. We started raising birds because we wanted to know exactly where our food came from, and we're glad to share that with our neighbors.

We're small on purpose. That means we know our flock, we answer our own messages, and every order goes through our hands.

How we raise our birds

What "cage-free" and "free-range" mean here

Cage-free

Our chickens and ducks are never kept in cages. They move around freely in their coops and runs.

Free-range

Our birds have access to the outdoors, so they can spend time outside instead of indoors all day.

Not organic

Our eggs are not certified organic, and we won't call them that. If you have questions about feed or care, just ask.

Chickens and ducks

We keep both, so we can offer everyday chicken eggs and harder-to-find duck eggs.
