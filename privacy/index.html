Privacy note

We keep this simple.

What we collect

When you use our order or contact form, we receive what you type in: your name, phone, email, address (if you ask for delivery) and your message. The form is delivered to our inbox by a form service called FormSubmit.

How we use it

Only to answer you and fill your order. We don't sell or share your information, and we won't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Website analytics

We may use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages are useful. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.

Questions or removal

Want us to delete your information? Contact us and we'll take care of it.
