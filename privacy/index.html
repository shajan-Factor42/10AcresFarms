Privacy note.

We keep this simple.

What we collect

When you use our order form or sign up for the egg list, we receive what you type in: your name, phone, email, delivery address and any notes. The forms are delivered to our inbox by a form service called FormSubmit.

How we use it

Only to fill your order, deliver your eggs, and send the egg list if you signed up. We don't sell or share your information.

Website analytics

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It uses cookies and doesn't tell us who you are.

Questions or removal

Want off the egg list, or want us to delete your information? Reply to any email from us and we'll take care of it.
