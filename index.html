How we raise them

Plain promises we can keep.

Cage-free

Our hens and ducks are never kept in cages. They sleep safe in our coop at night.

Free-range

Every day they're out on our land in Lawrenceville — scratching, foraging, and in the ducks' case, finding every puddle.

Family-raised

A small flock cared for by our family, not a factory. Questions about how we raise them? Just ask.

Straight answers: our eggs are cage-free and free-range. They are not certified organic, and we'll never label them that way.

Our farm

A family farm in Gwinnett County.

We're a family raising a small flock of chickens and ducks on our land in Lawrenceville. What started as a few birds for our own breakfast table turned into more eggs than we could eat, so now we share them with neighbors across Gwinnett County and Atlanta.

Our hens and ducks spend their days outside and sleep safe in the coop at night. If you ever have a question about the flock, ask us. You'll hear back from our family, not a call center.
