How we raise them

Plain promises we can keep.

Cage-free

Our hens and ducks are never kept in cages. They roost in [COOP / BARN] at night.

Free-range

Every day they're out on our land in Gwinnett County — scratching, foraging, and in the ducks' case, finding every puddle.

Collected by hand

We gather eggs every morning, clean and check each one, and stamp the carton with the date.

Straight answers: our eggs are cage-free and free-range. They are not certified organic, and we'll never label them that way.
