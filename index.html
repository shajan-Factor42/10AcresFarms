Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are your eggs organic?

No. Our eggs are cage-free and free-range, but they are not certified organic. We'd rather tell you exactly how our birds are raised than use a label we haven't earned.

How do I pay?

There's no payment online right now. Send an order request, we'll confirm it with you, and you pay when you pick up or when we deliver.

Do you deliver?

We deliver to some parts of Gwinnett County. Choose delivery on the order form and include your address. We'll let you know if you're in our delivery area.

What's the difference between duck eggs and chicken eggs?

Duck eggs are bigger, with thicker shells and a larger yolk compared to the white. Many bakers love them for cakes and custards. Read our full comparison.

Do you sell to stores and restaurants?

Yes. Tell us what you need on our contact page and we'll get back to you about wholesale.

Ready for fresh eggs?

Send an order request now. We'll confirm it with you personally.
